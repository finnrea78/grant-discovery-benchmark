Grants and Funding Opportunities for Individuals

We provide grants and funding opportunities to support research in British art. These are available to individuals at different stages of their careers. Applications for our Author Grants are now open, closing Monday 14 September.

  • Individual Research Grants

    Grants of up to £20,000 for individuals undertaking research travel and/or taking time to address specific research questions.

    link

  • Author Grants

    Grants of up to £6,000 for authors looking to publish written works reflecting advanced research in British art.

    link

  • Study Support Grants

    Grants of £5,000 for students seeking financial support to begin or continue their studies either via a Master’s level or Doctoral level degree.

    link

  • Andrew Wyld Research Support Grants

    Grants of £2,000 for students undertaking research into historical works on paper. Funded by the Andrew Wyld Fund.

    link

New Narratives Funding Opportunities

A set of opportunities specifically dedicated to expanding our understanding of British art through new perspectives. Applications for the Spring 2027 round will open Monday 30 November 2026.

  • New Narratives: Master's Degree Scholarship

    Support towards University fees and living costs for individuals looking to undertake a Master’s degree (MA, MPhil, MSc or equivalent).

    link

  • New Narratives: Doctoral Scholarship

    Support towards University fees and living costs, for three years, for individuals looking to undertake a doctorate in the field of British art.

    link

  • New Narratives: Early Career Fellowship

    Financial support for individuals looking to undertake a major project in the field of British art.

    link

  • New Narratives Film

    Watch our short film introducing our New Narratives scheme and recent recipients.

    link

Rome Fellowship

A three month residency at the British School at Rome for individuals undertaking advanced research on a British-Italian topic and who would benefit from an extended stay in Rome.

  • Rome Fellowship

    Allows the recipient to spend three months of dedicated research based at the British School at Rome (BSR).

    link

  • 125 Years of the British School at Rome

    2026 marks the 125th anniversary of the BSR. Find out more about the activities they have planned to celebrate.

    link

    Watch how Wilfried E. Keil, Rome Fellow in 2021, used his award to examine Robert Turnbull Macpherson’s original photographs of Rome.
