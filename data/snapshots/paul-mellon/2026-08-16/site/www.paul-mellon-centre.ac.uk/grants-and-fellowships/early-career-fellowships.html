New Narratives: Early Career Fellowship

Closed for applications

For individuals looking for funding to undertake an in-depth research project that will reshape our understanding of British art

Our New Narratives: Early Career Fellowship provides financial support to individuals looking to undertake a major project in the field of British art.

The Fellowship can either be taken for one year, at a total of £35,000, or for two years with a total of £70,000 (paid at £35,000 for each year).

The Fellowship is available to individuals who are at postdoctoral level (or who have equivalent experience) or above.

Applications are currently closed, the next round will be Spring 2027 with applications opening on Monday 30 November.

Funding and Benefits

Funding

This Fellowship is a fixed amount of:

£35,000 if taken as a one year Fellowship
£70,000 (£35,000 per year) if taken as a two year Fellowship

The Fellowship can be either paid directly to you as a grant or to your employer if you would prefer to take the funding as staff replacement costs for a period of leave.

We do not require you to provide a detailed budget; however, a comprehensive plan for activity across the two years should be included in the application form.

Benefits

The Centre’s Public Study Room will be open to you to use as a working environment if required. You will also be encouraged to attend our academic events programme, and to participate in the life of the Paul Mellon Centre (PMC) more generally.

We also host an annual lunch at the Centre where you can meet with staff and other recipients of the New Narratives awards.

To encourage networking and the exchanging of ideas, an extra £500 per year will be available to you for the duration of the award which you can put towards facilitating meetings with other individuals in the field who may provide mentorship, advice or collaborative opportunities for their research or project.

Eligibility

Applicant
Our New Narratives: Early Career Fellowship is available to individuals who have recently completed their doctorate degree, or who have equivalent research experience.

These Fellowships are open to international applicants.

Research Focus

This award is part of our New Narratives, a set of funding opportunities designed to increase the diversity of perspectives among researchers and students within the field of British art. As such the Advisory Council will take into account your academic background but also your intent to pursue research, activities or a career pathway that contributes to the reframing of British art and that challenge the received histories of British art.

More information on our funding criteria is available on our Our Funding Focus webpage and we have also highlighted some of our previously funded projects here.

You are expected to begin your Fellowship at some point during the calendar year it is awarded; deferrals are not permitted unless in extenuating circumstances.

Application Content

Your application should include:

  • a detailed outline of the research project with details on how your project will significantly contribute to the wider British art field
  • a detailed timetable of research activity
  • a demonstration of how you intend to pursue research, activities or a career pathway that contributes to the reframing of British art
  • one academic or professional reference

We have provided specific Application Guidance and an example Application Form for download below.

The number of good applications is likely to exceed the funding available, so the Advisory Council are advised to use some or all of the following criteria to help them in making decisions on how best to allocate funding:

  • academic rigour
  • originality and distinctness in the context of current scholarship
  • contribution to scholarship in the longer term
  • timeliness, where a project is connected with a particular moment or opportunity
  • where a grant would support work in a geographical or cultural context which has not hitherto been supported by PMC
  • need, where the research would be unlikely to go ahead without this support
  • impact and inclusion, where the research would include voices or reach a broader range of people than have hitherto been represented in the field of British art studies
  • feasibility and quality of application; the information provided is thorough, clear and demonstrates that the project can be undertaken in the time and manner proposed
  • value for money and environmental impact; the research has a realistic and appropriate budget and environmental impacts have been given consideration (eg. alternative forms of transport)

You should read our Frequently Asked Questions and Our Funding Focus webpages carefully to ensure eligibility and to make your application as strong as possible.

Application Proccess

  1. Submit Your Application
    All applications must be submitted via grants.paul-mellon-centre.ac.uk. Once registered you will be able to select the relevant opportunity and fill out the application form, this will include providing the details of your referee.
  2. Referee
    After your application has been submitted, we will contact your referee directly. Applicants are responsible for ensuring their referee submits their reference by the deadline. The deadline for doing so will be communicated with you and the referee.
  3. Assessment
    We undertake a two-round assessment process. In the first round all applications are read and assessed by a Sub-Committee of our Advisory Council members. A percentage of the top scoring applications will then proceed to the second and final round of assessment, involving all of our Advisory Council members, which will conclude in a decision meeting where the grants are awarded.
  4. Final Decision
    We will keep you updated about the status of your application throughout the assessment process and will inform all applicants of the final decisions by the date given at the time of application.

Restrictions

  • If you are in receipt of an Early Career Fellowship then you cannot hold another paid award or fellowship that runs concurrently.

Access

Accessible Formats: We can provide large text or clear text formats of our application forms and guidance upon request. If you have any other access requirements regarding our website or experience any issues using our online grants system then please contact us.

Access Funding: We are committed to making our opportunities accessible to everyone. To support this, we have set aside funding to help you if you may face additional barriers and as such incur extra costs whilst undertaking your research projects. If your application is successful, we will be happy to discuss how this funding can support your additional needs.

Recipient Requirements

  • If you are successful you will be asked to agree to our Terms & Conditions.
  • You will need to write an annual report for each year you receive funding as well as a final report, that details the research activity and impact of the funding, and submit this within three months of completing the award.
