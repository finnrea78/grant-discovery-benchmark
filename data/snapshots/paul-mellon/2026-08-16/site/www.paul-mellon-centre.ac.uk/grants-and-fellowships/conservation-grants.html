Grants & Funding Opportunities

Conservation Grants

Open for applications

Supporting organisations to undertake vital conservation research on items from their own collections

Our Conservation Grants are intended to help protect and preserve significant works of British art by providing organisations with funding to undertake original and needed conservation research.

The grants are for any amount up to £25,000.

Conservation Grants are available to any organisation, institution or nonprofit which is undertaking a conservation research project or technical analysis which will lead to a research project. Items supported by this grant must be in public or charitable ownership and accessible to the public.

Applications for the Autumn 2026 round of funding are now open, applications close on Monday 14 September. Apply here.

Funding

Organisations can claim for any amount up to £25,000 and all applicants will need to provide a detailed budget of how the funding will be spent (if exact costs are not known please include estimates). The application will need to include a clear and reasoned timeline and, although there are no restrictions regarding the length of the project, we would normally expect an outcome within five years of the award being made.

Costs that can be claimed for include:

Staff

  • replacement staff costs to give cover for a staff member to work on the project
  • freelancer rates for individuals to undertake research and work on the project
  • research travel and other research-related costs for individuals working on the project

Equipment

  • transportation and packing
  • specialist equipment, materials or software for digitisation of resources

Please note that no more than 33% of the total budget can be dedicated towards costs in the Equipment category – the majority should cover staff costs.

This list is not exhaustive however if you would like to claim for costs not listed above please do speak to our Grants & Fellowships Manager before making the application.

Outcomes

The support from this grant should result in a conservation research project that enhances the understanding of the object, the details of which should be provided in the final report.

Eligibility

Applicant
Our Conservation Grants are available to any organisations, institutions, nonprofits, or other entities which hold a dedicated business account. Collaborative applications between two or more organisations are welcomed; however, one partner must be identified as the lead organisation. Applications from internationally based organisations are also eligible.

Project
All applicants must demonstrate how the grant will support their original and innovative conservation research in the field of British art, visual culture and/or architecture. More information on our subject criteria is available on our Our Funding Focus webpage and we have also highlighted some of our previously funded projects here.

Items supported by this grant must be in public or charitable ownership and accessible to the public.

Application Content

For Conservation Grants your application should include:

  • a thorough outline of the research project
  • details on how the project will impact the wider British art field
  • a comprehensive timeline and budget
  • information on any ethical considerations relating to the project and how these would be addressed
  • one academic or professional reference relating to the key individual involved in the project

We have provided specific Application Guidance and an example Application Form for download below.

The number of good applications is likely to exceed the funding available, so the Advisory Council are advised to use some or all of the following criteria to help them in making decisions on how best to allocate funding:

  • academic rigour
  • originality and distinctness in the context of current scholarship
  • contribution to scholarship in the longer term
  • timeliness, where a project is connected with a particular moment or opportunity
  • where a grant would support work in a geographical or cultural context which has not hitherto been supported by PMC
  • need, where the research would be unlikely to go ahead without this support
  • impact and inclusion, where the research would include voices or reach a broader range of people than have hitherto been represented in the field of British art studies
  • feasibility and quality of application; the information provided is thorough, clear and demonstrates that the project can be undertaken in the time and manner proposed
  • value for money and environmental impact; the research has a realistic and appropriate budget and environmental impacts have been given consideration (eg. alternative forms of transport)

You should read our Frequently Asked Questions and our Our Funding Focus webpages carefully to ensure eligibility and to make your application as strong as possible.

The application requires one referee.

Application Process

  1. Submit Your Application
    All applications must be submitted via grants.paul-mellon-centre.ac.uk. Once registered you will be able to select the relevant opportunity and fill out the application form, this will include providing the details of your referee.
  2. Referee
    After your application has been submitted, we will contact your referee directly. Applicants are responsible for ensuring their referee submits their reference by the deadline. The deadline for doing so will be communicated to you and the referee.
  3. Assessment
    We undertake a two-round assessment process. In the first round all applications are read and assessed by a subcommittee of our Advisory Council members. A percentage of the top-scoring applications will then proceed to the second and final round of assessment, involving all of our Advisory Council members, which will conclude in a decision meeting where the grants are awarded.
  4. Final Decision
    We will keep you updated about the status of your application throughout the assessment process and will inform all applicants of the final decisions by the date given at the time of application.

Restrictions

  • Applications can only be made by organisations seeking to undertake work on their own collections.
  • Only one Conservation Research Grant application is permitted per organisation per round.

Access

Accessible Formats: We can provide large text or clear text formats of our application forms and guidance upon request. If you have any other access requirements regarding our website or experience any issues using our online grants system then please contact us.

Access Funding: We are committed to making our opportunities accessible to everyone. To support this, we have set aside funding to help individuals who may face additional barriers and as such incur extra costs whilst undertaking their research projects. If your application is successful, we will be happy to discuss how this funding can support your additional needs.

Recipient Requirements

  • If successful then the recipient will need to adhere to our Terms and Conditions. This includes providing a thorough final report within three months of completing the award term.
  • The organisation should acknowledge the support of the Paul Mellon Centre through any materials associated with the project, full guidelines for acknowledgment and copies of the logo will be provided.
