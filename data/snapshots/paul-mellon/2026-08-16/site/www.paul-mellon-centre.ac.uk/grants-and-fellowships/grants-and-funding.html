Grants and Funding Opportunities

We award around £1 million each year to inspiring research projects in the field of British art, visual culture and architectural history. Our funding is offered through dedicated grants and funding opportunities for individual and organisational applicants.

Applications are now open for our Autumn 2026 round of funding opportunities. This round includes all of our grants for organisations as well as our Author Grants for individuals. Applications close on Monday 14 September.
