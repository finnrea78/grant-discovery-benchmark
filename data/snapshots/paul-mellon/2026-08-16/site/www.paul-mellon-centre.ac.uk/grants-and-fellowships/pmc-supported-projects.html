Our Supported Projects & People

From short research trips to complex curatorial programming, we believe in supporting the generation of ideas- however that support is needed. Below, you can explore some of the people, places, publications and projects we have supported so far.

  • Featured Project: A Global History of Victorian Painting: Circulations and the Making of British Art

    Kate Nichols was awarded £18,000 towards replacement staff costs that allowed her to focus on her ongoing book project.

    link

  • Featured Report: Curating the Sea

    Sarah Wade and Pandora Syperek received a grant of £20,000 for a collaborative research on curatorial approaches to the oceans.

    link

People

From postgraduate study to career-defining investigation, we support individuals to undertake research through dedicated trips or sustained periods of research time.

  • Sonal Khullar awarded £18,000 in 2024

    To cover staff replacement costs for Sonal Khullar’s post, allowing her to dedicate a term to research on her forthcoming book.

    link

  • Cassandra Harrington awarded £2,000

    To support research trips to study carvings, manuscripts and archival material held in several libraries and historic buildings in the UK in 2024.

    link

  • Sarah Churchill awarded £15,000 in 2024

    To support a long-term project and develop her doctoral thesis for a book proposal.

    link

  • Aindreas Scholz awarded £783.00 in 2024

    To support research trips that contextualised Anna Atkins’s life in Victorian Britain as well as to see, and connect with, Atkins’s own books.

    link

Places

Organisations across the British Isles and beyond have received support for conservation programmes, research projects and curatorial activities. Explore the public programming realised by our funding.

  • Exhibition: Pop and the Figure

    Showing Pallant House Gallery's distinguished Pop Art collection through the lens of the body until Spring 2027.

    link

  • Conference: Harmondsworth Barn 600th Anniversary

    A conference exploring new research into medieval vernacular buildings from the site of the largest surviving medieval timber-framed barn in England.

    link

  • Event: Globe-spanning Networks: Mapping Amateur Photographic Clubs and Societies in Local, National, and Transnational Contexts

    Conference, Birkbeck, 26th November 2026.

    link

  • Exhibition: Balraj Khanna

    In autumn 2026, Ikon presents the first major survey exhibition of artist Balraj Khanna (1939-2024).

    link

Projects

We support the sharing of knowledge about British art to create better understandings of the past, present, and potential futures. Online, our recipients illustrate the breadth of innovation in the field.

  • Joseph Wright of Derby

    The online home of Derby Museums’ Joseph Wright Collection.

    link

  • The Jeffrey Sherwin & Family Collection of British Surrealism

    The Hepworth Wakefield's dedicated webpage for the newly acquired collection, with images of works, including in-depth research.

    link

  • Exhibiting Empire in Nineteenth Century Liverpool

    An online resource containing digitised items relating to the history of art, manufacturing, British imperialism, and exhibition culture in nineteenth century Liverpool.

    link

  • Black Women Poets at Sheba Feminist Publishers

    A blog post on items from the Sheba Feminist Publishers archive by Doctoral Scholarship recipient Nazmia Jamal.
