Grants & Funding Opportunities

Individual Research Grants

Closed for applications

For individuals undertaking research travel and/or taking time to address specific research questions

Our Individual Research Grants provide flexible financial support to allow individuals to undertake research in the field of British art. This might involve supporting research time (including through backfill for those in employment) and/or research travel to view collections or visit archives, as well as some additional costs.

The grants are tiered and are available as per below:

  • Up to £3,000
    Available to individuals at any stage of their career.
  • £3,001-£8,000
    Available to individuals at any stage of their career.
  • £8,001-£20,000
    Available to individuals who are at a postdoctoral level, or who have equivalent research experience, or above.

Applications are currently closed, the next round will be Spring 2027 with applications opening on Monday 30 November.

Funding

The costs claimed must be clearly defined in the budget section of the application form and justification for the amount claimed should be given.

You are encouraged to budget realistically and provide a clear reasoning for all expenses, to maximise your chances of success.

There is no fixed duration or timeframe in which the travel and/or research period needs to take place; this can be determined by you. We do however expect the activity to be completed within two years of the award being granted unless otherwise specified in the application form.

This grant can go towards:

Research Trips

You can apply for costs to support undertaking travel for research purposes (eg. to visit archives, sites of interest for research purposes).

Costs claimed could include:

  • Travel (UK or international)
  • Accommodation
  • Daily subsistence

There are no budget limitations to these costs; however we would expect them to be reasonable depending on where the travel is being undertaken. We encourage you to consider the environmental impact your choice of travel when planning your trips, and to reflect this thinking in your proposal.

Please include a draft itinerary of the trip in your application form as well as a detailed budget (example costs are permitted if exact costs aren’t known).

Research Time

You can also apply to support a period of focused and sustained research towards your project.

Costs claimed can go towards:

  • staff replacement costs: if you are in employment
  • daily rates: if you are freelance or taking leave from employment to undertake research
  • supporting costs: a portion of the budget can also go towards costs to facilitate and support your research eg. research materials, copying fees, subscriptions to online resources. These costs should be clearly reasoned and no more than 10% of the overall budget should be allocated to these.

You are permitted to include elements of both areas of purpose in your proposal, eg. a period of focused research which includes some travel.

Applications from individuals who wish to take the grant for research on a part-time basis are permitted.

Other Costs

This list is not exhaustive but if you would like to claim for costs not listed above please do speak to our Grants Manager before submitting the application.

Guidance regarding costs relating to access needs can be found below under Access.

This grant can either be paid directly to the recipient or the recipient's nominated institution/organisation if they intend to take the grant as staff replacement costs.

Eligibility

Applicant
Our Individual Research Grants are available to individuals depending on the career stages as per below:

  • Up to £3,000
    Available to individuals at any stage of their career.
  • £3,001-£8,000
    Available to individuals at any stage of their career.
  • £8,001-£20,000
    Available to individuals who are at postdoctoral level, or who have equivalent research experience, or above.

These grants are open to international applicants.

Project
The subject of the research must meet our criteria, more information on this is available on our Our Funding Focus webpage and we have also highlighted some of our previously funded projects here.

You must demonstrate how the grant will enhance your research on British art, visual culture and/or architecture.

Outcomes

This grant is designed to be flexible in scope and to provide funding to support you in undertaking research towards a specific project.

You need to specify a distinct outcome or detail how the funded period will significantly contribute to an outcome or larger research project. This could include a publication, series of articles or exhibition proposal.

This grant is designed to support the facilitation of research and is not designed to support costs specifically towards the outcome, please see instead our Author Grants or Exhibition Publication Grants to support the practical costs of publishing or our Research Project Grants if you are part of an organisation seeking curatorial research support.

Application Content

Your application should include:

  • a detailed project outline
  • a thorough timetable of research activity and/or an itinerary for the research trip
  • a comprehensive budget (example costs permitted)
  • one academic or professional reference

We have provided specific Application Guidance and an example Application Form for download below.

The number of good applications is likely to exceed the funding available, so the Advisory Council are advised to use some or all of the following criteria to help them in making decisions on how best to allocate funding:

  • academic rigour
  • originality and distinctness in the context of current scholarship
  • contribution to scholarship in the longer term
  • timeliness, where a project is connected with a particular moment or opportunity
  • where a grant would support work in a geographical or cultural context which has not hitherto been supported by PMC
  • need, where the research would be unlikely to go ahead without this support
  • impact and inclusion, where the research would include voices or reach a broader range of people than have hitherto been represented in the field of British art studies
  • feasibility and quality of application; the information provided is thorough, clear and demonstrates that the project can be undertaken in the time and manner proposed
  • value for money and environmental impact; the research has a realistic and appropriate budget and environmental impacts have been given consideration (eg. alternative forms of transport)

You should read our Frequently Asked Questions and Our Funding Focus webpages carefully to ensure eligibility and to make your application as strong as possible.

Application Process

  1. Submit Your Application
    All applications must be filled out via grants.paul-mellon-centre.ac.uk. Once registered you will be able to select the relevant opportunity and fill out the application form, this will include providing the details of your referee.
  2. Referee
    After your application has been submitted, we will contact your referee directly. Applicants are responsible for ensuring their referee submits their reference by the deadline. The deadline for doing so will be communicated with you and the referee.
  3. Assessment
    We undertake a two-round assessment process. In the first round all applications are read and assessed by a Sub-Committee of our Advisory Council members. A percentage of the top scoring applications will then proceed to the second and final round of assessment, involving all of our Advisory Council members, which will conclude in a decision meeting where the grants are awarded.
  4. Final Decision
    We will keep you updated about the status of your application throughout the assessment process and will inform all applicants of the final decisions by the date given at the time of application. Due to the high volume of applications, individual feedback is not guaranteed, though specific requests may be accommodated.

Restrictions

  • Applications from organisations (on behalf of individuals) are not accepted.
  • Projects that are part of an organisations programme (e.g. exhibitions) are not accepted.
  • If you are in receipt of an Individual Research Grant then you cannot hold another paid award or fellowship that runs concurrently.

Access

Accessible Formats: We can provide large text or clear text formats of our application forms and guidance upon request. If you have any other access requirements regarding our website or experience any issues using our online grants system then please contact us.

Access Funding: We are committed to making our opportunities accessible to everyone. To support this, we have set aside funding to help you if you may face additional barriers and as such incur extra costs whilst undertaking your research projects. If your application is successful, we will be happy to discuss how this funding can support your additional needs.

Recipient Requirements

  • If you are successful you will be asked to agree to our Terms & Conditions.
  • You will need to write a final report, that details the research activity and impact of the funding, and submit this within three months of completing the award.
