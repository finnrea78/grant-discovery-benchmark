Art Teachers Connect: Connections Bursary 

  • 13 December 2024

We are delighted to announce the launch of a Connections Bursary: a new part of the Art Teachers Connect (ATC) programme the Paul Mellon Centre co-delivers with the University of Leeds. Secondary school art teachers, who are members of the ATC community, can apply for support towards opportunities that will enhance their teaching of art and art history.

The bursary will be available to cover activities such as going to a talk or conference, visiting another ATC teacher at their school to observe their teaching and share ideas, or seeing an exhibition, collection or individual at a museum/gallery/arts space.

In 2025 there will be two rounds of funding: Spring and Summer.

The Spring round is currently open and the deadline for applications is 10am GMT, Monday 24 February.

The Summer round will open in May 2025 and the deadline for applications will be 10am BST, Monday 2 June.

Visit atcuk.org for more information.
