Start your application

You can apply for our funding for places through our online application system.

We have recently moved to a new online application system, provided by Blackbaud.

To apply for one of our grant programmes, please select the appropriate link below. You will need to create an account before starting your application if you don’t already have a Blackbaud login.

Science & Medicine
Health & Disability
Heritage, Humanities & the Arts

If you are a secondary school or sixth form college, please visit our secondary schools and sixth form colleges funding page for more information about the programme, including how and when to apply. 

To help you submit your application, we have put together information on using our online application system. This covers questions including creating an account, linking your organisation, and completing the application form.

For more information on our funding criteria and to check if your project is eligible, visit our funding for places pages. On these pages you can also see key dates and deadlines under the ‘when to apply’ section.

If you’d like to discuss your funding proposal before starting an application, please contact us.

Privacy overview

This website uses cookies. Cookie information is stored in your browser and performs functions such as recognising you when you return to our website and helping us to understand which sections of the website you find most useful and interesting.

You can adjust all of your cookie settings by navigating the tabs on the left hand side.

Cookie notice

The Cookie notice is used in order to save your preferences for cookie settings and whether or not to continue showing you the cookie notice.

Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics to collect anonymous information such as the number of visitors to the site, and the most popular pages.

Keeping this cookie enabled helps us to improve our website for our visitors.

Cookie policy

The majority of websites you visit will use some form of cookies in order to improve your user experience. This might be for the duration of your visit (by using what’s called a ‘session cookie’) or for repeat visits (by using a ‘persistent cookie’). Cookies are little bits of code that are placed on your computer.
