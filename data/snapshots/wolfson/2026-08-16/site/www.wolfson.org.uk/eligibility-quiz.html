We are not able to fund the following:

  • Purchase of land or existing buildings (including a building’s freehold)
  • Overheads, running costs, salaries, maintenance costs and VAT
  • Non-specific appeals (including circulars) and endowment funds
  • Projects with a shortfall of less than £15,000
  • Facilities for primary or pre-school age children, except for those providing palliative care
  • Playgrounds and play equipment
  • Sport pitches and facilities
  • Sensory rooms, sensory therapy or equipment for sensory play
  • Leisure, holiday schemes or respite breaks
  • Costs of meetings, exhibitions, concerts, expeditions, conferences, lectures, etc.
  • Film, websites or promotional materials
  • New organisations that have not yet filed two years of annual accounts
  • Community Interest Companies
  • Multi-purpose spaces such as community or multi-arts centres
  • Charities where support for older people, people with mental health problems or disabilities is only a minor part of the services provided (for organisations applying under our Health & Disability programme)

If your organisation falls into more than one place category, please select the option which most closely matches. If your organisation type is not in the list, it is very unlikely that you will be eligible for our funding.

We do not give grants directly to individuals. Our funding for people is administered through our partners. Visit our funding for people page for more information.
