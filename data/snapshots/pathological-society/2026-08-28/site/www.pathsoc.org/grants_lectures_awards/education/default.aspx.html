    Education Grants

    1. Home
    2. Grants, Lectures & Awards
    3. Education Grants

    The Education grants, bursaries and prizes are designed to further the interests of the Society through the support and development of pathology focused undergraduate and postgraduate educational/training activities.  Funding opportunities are available for students, supervisors and educational groups (e.g. student societies).

    Details of all Education Grants can be found here. 

    All grants and bursaries currently open for applications are listed below. If a scheme does not appear on this list, or if the "apply" option is not available and dates not updated, the scheme is not yet open. Please check our Education Grants list for full details.
