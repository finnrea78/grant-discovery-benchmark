    Student Society Bursary Scheme

    1. Home
    2. Student Society Bursary Scheme

    About the Grant

    Support for the promotion of the understanding of pathology amongst medical, dental and biomedical students

    The sum requested should not exceed £500.00

    Who is eligible?

    Established student societies where the applicant is a member of the Society based in UK &/or Ireland

    Deadline Dates

    Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis.

    How do I apply?

    Please email a covering letter, one page A4 application explaining  the proposal and justifying the proposed expenditure, one page description of the student Society and its aims & a written and signed endorsement by a member of the Pathological Society in good standing for 12 months

    Please read the full terms and conditions before applying

    To apply for undergraduate membership click here
