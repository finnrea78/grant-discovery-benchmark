    Travel Grants

    1. Home
    2. Grants, Lectures & Awards
    3. Travel Grants

    The Pathological Society offers a number of bursaries to support registering for and travelling to meetings, including bursaries for trainees and undergraduates to attend the summer and winter society meetings.

    Travel grants are available for both our Winter and Summer Meetings. Applications usually open once the programmes have been finalised and published. If a specific travel bursary is not visible on this page, this means it is not yet open for applications.

    Please see the following currently available options:

    • Travel & Conference Bursary 2026 (external events)
    • Winter Pathological Society Travel Bursary for Concessionary (with RSM) 2027
    • Winter Pathological Society Travel Bursary for Undergraduates/FD (with RSM) 2027
