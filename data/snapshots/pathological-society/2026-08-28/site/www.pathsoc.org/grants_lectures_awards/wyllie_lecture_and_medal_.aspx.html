    Wyllie Lecture and Medal

    1. Home
    2. Grants, Lectures & Awards
    3. Lectures & Awards
    4. Wyllie Lecture and Medal

    The Wyllie Lecture & Medal has been established to mark the death of Professor Andrew Wyllie (2022) together with the 50 year anniversary (1972) of the seminal paper of Kerr, Wyllie and Currie that first described apoptosis, a major historical event for British pathology and the Pathological Society. Andrew Wyllie was President of the Pathological Society (2010). This lecture will be given by an early to mid-career academic pathologist (by application), as Andrew Wyllie always encouraged early and mid-career academic pathologists.

    Nomination/application deadline of 1 August 2026 for award at January 2027 Winter Meeting.
