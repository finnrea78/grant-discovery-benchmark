    Undergraduate Elective Bursary UB0426

    1. Home
    2. Grants, Lectures & Awards

    About the Grant

    *This bursary is now closed for applications and this page is provided for information purposes only. Please note that the Terms & Conditions may be updated before the next application round opens. Applicants should review the guidance and Terms & Conditions again before submitting a future application.

    Funding to support research projects in pathology or gain experience in diagnostic departments during elective or vacation periods

    £220.00 per week for a maximum of eight weeks

    Who is eligible?

    Undergraduate Society members who are students of medicine, veterinary medicine, dentistry or biomedical science based in UK and/or Irelandnd

    Deadline Dates

    28 February and 28 April

    The following documents will need to be uploaded during the application process:

    Part B

    Please read the full terms and conditions before applying
