    JSPS Intermediate Research Fellowship 10/26

    1. Home
    2. Grants, Lectures & Awards
    3. JSPS Intermediate Research Fellowship 10/26

    Current Application Deadline: 1 October 2026 23:59

    About the Grant

    To support newly accredited pathologists during the period between completing their training (passing FRCPath part 2/completing the Certificate of Completion of Training) and obtaining full funding for a Clinician Scientist post through MRC, Wellcome, CRUK etc.

    The grant provides up to £120,000 over a maximum of 2 years, and is intended to cover 50% of salary costs (with 50% of salary provided by the NHS) and up to £20K consumables per year.

    Who is eligible

    Applicants must have a PhD/MD and have completed clinical training at the time of taking up the award in either Histopathology or Dental / Oral Pathology. Applicants should have been a member of the Society for at least 12 months.

    Deadline Date

    01 April & 01 October
