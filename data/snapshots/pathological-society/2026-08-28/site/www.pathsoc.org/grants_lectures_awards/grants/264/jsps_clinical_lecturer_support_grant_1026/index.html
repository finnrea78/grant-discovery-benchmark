    JSPS Clinical Lecturer Support Grant 10/26

    1. Home
    2. Grants, Lectures & Awards
    3. JSPS Clinical Lecturer Support Grant 10/26

    Current Application Deadline: 1 October 2026 23:59

    About the Grant

    To offer support for continuing research projects over a 2 year period by academic trainee histopathologists following the award of a PhD or MD, when progressing through the final stages of histopatholgical training leading up to the award of CCT. 

    £60,000 per annum funding over a 2 year period, to be used flexibly to fund the costs of employing a research technician or assistant and associated consumable costs.

    Who is eligible

    Trainee members of the Society for at least 2 years who have completed a PhD or MD usually within the preceeding 5 years and be in a recognised Specialist trainee (normally ST3 and above) histopathology programme accredited for the award of a CCT in histopathology in Great Britain & Ireland

    Deadline Date

    01 April and 1 October
