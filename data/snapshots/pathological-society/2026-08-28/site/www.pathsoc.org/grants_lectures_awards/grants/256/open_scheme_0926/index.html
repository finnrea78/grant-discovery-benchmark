    Open Scheme 09/26

    1. Home
    2. Grants, Lectures & Awards
    3. Open Scheme 09/26

    Current Application Deadline: 1 September 2026 23:59

    About the Grant

    Support for activities not covered by another Society scheme, all of which should support the Society's mission

    Who is eligible?

    Members of the Society in good standing for at least 12 months. Non members may also apply with the support of a Society member.

    Deadline Dates

    1 March, 1 June, 1 September & 1 December

    The following documents will need to be uploaded during the application process:

    Covering letter, 2 page A4 application explaining the proposal and justifying the proposed expenditure, 1 page CV of the principal applicant

    Please read the full terms and conditions before applying

    Apply for this grant

    Examples of Projects funded from the Open Scheme

    National Undergraduate Pathology Conference 2025 - Miss Jeong Hyun (Joy) Yoon, Imperial College London

    UK Lynch Syndrome Research Working Group Meeting - 0921 Dr M Jansen, UCL 

    To support the upkeep and maintenance of SEARCHBreast, a virtual on-line resource to locate and share surplus archival material from breast cancer animal models to help address the 3Rs and to promote 3D tissue modelling - Prof Val Speirs, University of Aberdeen (formerly University of Leeds)

    Purchase of Down Draft Table for Pathology teaching along with Donation Plaque - Dr E Benjamin, UCL
