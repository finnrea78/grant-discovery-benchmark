    Education Grant Scheme 10/26

    1. Home
    2. Grants, Lectures & Awards
    3. Education Grant Scheme 10/26

    Current Application Deadline: 1 October 2026 23:59

    About the Grant

    Funding to support a range of activities in the performance, development and assessment of undergraduate & postgraduate education in pathology.

    The sum requested should not exceed £5,000.

    Who is eligible?

    Members of the Society who have been in membership for a minimum of 12 months.

    Deadline Dates

    1 April & 1 October

    The following documents will need to be uploaded during the application process:

    Covering letter, 2 page A4 application explaining the proposal and justifying the proposed expenditure, 1 page CV of the principal applicant.

    Please read the full terms and conditions before applying.

    Apply for this grant

    Examples of Projects funded from the Education Grant

    An action research approach to develop a novel 'flipped delivery' of pathology teaching, Dr R Ganesan, Birmingham Women's Hospital & Mrs, Sara Smith, University of Wolverhampton

    2010 UK Cardiac Pathology Network - Annual Training Day Dr M Burke

    Online Collaboration of Northern Ireland Pathologists Network – Redstar – to provide weekly Black Box cases for trainee pathologists, Prof G McCluggage

    Midland CELLibration, a biennial weekend symposium, held by the Central Cellular Pathology Society (CCPS), Dr S Gawthorpe

    Promotion of Pathology in undergraduate and postgraduate education, both medical and non-medical, and secondary education, Dr L Hannan and Prof A Howie

    2013 UK Cardiac Pathology Network, Dr H Doran
