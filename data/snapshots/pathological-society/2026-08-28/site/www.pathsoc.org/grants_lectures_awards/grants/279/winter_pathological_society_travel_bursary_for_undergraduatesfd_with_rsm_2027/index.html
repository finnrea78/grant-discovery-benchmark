    Winter Pathological Society Travel Bursary for Undergraduates/FD (with RSM) 2027

    1. Home
    2. Grants, Lectures & Awards
    3. Winter Pathological Society Travel Bursary for Undergraduates/FD (with RSM) 2027

    Current Application Deadline: 20 December 2026 23:59

    About the grant

    To cover costs of up to £350 to attend a Winter Pathological Society meeting. This bursary is intended to contribute towards travel, accommodation and registration fees only. All other expenses, including meals, refreshments and other incidental or personal costs, are not eligible for reimbursement. 

    Who is eligible?

    Undergraduate and Foundation Doctor members of the Society.

    Members who have been awarded a PathSoc travel bursary within the last 6 months from the application date are ineligible.  

    Deadline Dates

    20 December for the Path Society Winter Meeting, January of the following year (see event calendar for meeting dates)

     

    Please read the full terms and conditions before applying. Please note registration for the Meeting is not automatically done upon approval of this bursary. You will need to register to attend. Receipts should be uploaded with your application; if any are missing, estimated costs are accepted and receipts can be uploaded later.

    How do I apply?
