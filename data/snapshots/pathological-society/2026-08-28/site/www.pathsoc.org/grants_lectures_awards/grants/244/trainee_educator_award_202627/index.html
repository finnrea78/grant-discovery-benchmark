    TRAINEE EDUCATOR AWARD (TEA) 2026-27

    *This is now closed for nominations and this page is provided for information purposes only. Please note that the Terms & Conditions may be updated before the next application round opens. Applicants should review the guidance and Terms & Conditions again before submitting a future nomination.

    The Trainee Educator Award (TEA) is your chance to recognise and reward an excellent pathology registrar / resident who has provided high quality educational or training activities in pathology. 

    The future of pathology relies on the passing on of knowledge and the purpose of this award is to provide recognition for excellent education and training in pathology and to ensure the continuation of the mission of the Pathological Society of Great Britain and Ireland.

    Applications are encouraged from current pathology trainees and consultants to nominate a pathology registrar / resident who has excelled in delivering educational activities or content for others. The following key areas in applications will be assessed:

    • Impact & benefit to others
    • Leadership
    • Resourcefulness
    • Engagement & communication.

    The award is comprised of a Trainee Educator medal & £100 book voucher prize for the winning trainee.

    Guidance on submission

    Please complete an application via the link below by deadline 3rd July 2026, 23:59 (BST).

    We encourage institutions to send a group nomination rather than submit multiple individual nominations.

    Terms & conditions

    1. The nominating person must be a member of the Society and based in the UK or Republic of Ireland. If they are a pathology trainee, they must have a national training number (NTN) or equivalent at the time of application. This includes out of programme trainees.

    2. The nominated registrar / resident does not need to be a member of the Society but must be based in the UK or Republic of Ireland.

    3. The nominated registrar / resident must be working in one of the branches of Cellular Pathology. 

    4. The nominated registrar / resident must have delivered or facilitated educational or training activities within the last 12 months.

    5. This award is limited to one nomination per person per year.

    6. If multiple trainees submit separate nominations for the same registrar/resident, only the highest-scoring application will be considered in determining the final outcome.

    7. A medal and £100 book voucher will be given to the award winner.

    8. Nominations will be judged by the Pathological Society Educational Subcommittee.  

    9. If a proposal is successful, the same individual is prohibited from nominating the same trainee educator the following year. However, nominations from the same trainee for a different trainee educator are welcomed, as are nominations for the same trainee educator by a different trainee.

    10. Registrars/ residents who have not won in the previous years may be renominated.

    Winners

    2024
