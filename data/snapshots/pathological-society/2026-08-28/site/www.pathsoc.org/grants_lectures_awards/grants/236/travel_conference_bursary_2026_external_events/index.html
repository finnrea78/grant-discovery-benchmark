    Travel & Conference Bursary 2026 (external events)

    1. Home
    2. Grants, Lectures & Awards
    3. Travel & Conference Bursary 2026 (external events)

    About the grant

    To cover the costs of travel and/or registration fees to promote scientific communications at major international meetings. This bursary is for external events only and cannot be used for Pathological Society Meetings. If you wish to apply for a Path Soc Joint Meeting (Winter or Summer), please apply via "Travel Grants" and select the appropriate bursary. Applications submitted here for Path Soc Meetings will be rejected.

    The Travel & Conference bursary is up to £500.00 and must supplement monies from other sources.

    The Travel Bursary is intended to contribute towards travel, accommodation and registration fees only. All other expenses, including meals, refreshments and other incidental or personal costs, are not eligible for reimbursement.

    Who is eligible?

    Concessionary members of the Society in good standing for 12 months and Undergraduate/Foundation Doctor members of the Society.

    Members who have been awarded a PathSoc travel bursary within the last 6 months from the application date are ineligible.  

    Deadline Dates

    No deadline - ongoing

     

    Please read the full terms and conditions before applying. Receipts should be uploaded with your application; if any are missing, estimated costs are accepted and receipts can be uploaded later.
