    Sir Nicholas Wright Lecture

    1. Home
    2. Grants, Lectures & Awards
    3. Lectures & Awards
    4. Sir Nicholas Wright Lecture

    The Sir Nicholas Wright Lecture was founded in 2016/2017 in honour of Professor Sir Nicholas Wright (previous President of The Pathological Society and major contributor to UK pathological research).

    The objective of the Wright Lecture is to stimulate original investigative studies in the fields of cellular and molecular pathology and to encourage and reward young cellular pathology trainees in their research efforts 

    Nomination/application deadline of 1 December 2025 for award at the 2026 Summer Meeting.  

    Deadline for nominations has been extended to Thursday 15 January 2026.
