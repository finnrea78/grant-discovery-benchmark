    Research Grants

    1. Home
    2. Grants, Lectures & Awards
    3. Research Grants

    The research grants are designed to further the interests of the Society through the support of research activities of the membership. Full details of the research grants can be found in the sub-menu to the right (or at the top of the screen, on mobile devices).

    The Pathological Society of Great Britain and Ireland is a National Institute for Health Research (NIHR) non-commercial Partner, Society-funded studies may be elibible to access the NIHR study Support Service which is provided by the NIHR Clinical Reserch Network. To find out more, please visit: https://dev.nihr.ac.uk/explore-nihr/support/study-support-service.htm 

    For a schematic that details the various grants available at different stages of training please click here 

    Full list of research grants can be found here.
