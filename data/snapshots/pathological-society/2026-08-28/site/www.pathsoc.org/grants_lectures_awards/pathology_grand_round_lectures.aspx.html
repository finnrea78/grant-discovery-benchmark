    Upcoming Lecture

    Date: Wednesday, 16 September 2026, 09:00 - 10:00 (UK time) 

    Joining Details for all Participants (Path Soc Members and Non-Members): Webinar Registration - Zoom link     

    Speaker: Professor Trevor Graham, Director of the Centre for Evolution and Cancer: Genomics and Evolutionary Dynamics (ICR).  

    Title: Preventing colorectal cancer in inflammatory bowel disease.

    Abstract: 

    Inflammatory bowel disease (IBD) is common, affecting around 1 in 100 people (0.5M) in the UK. People with IBD have a significantly increased risk of colorectal cancer (CRC). I will discuss our work studying the clonal evolution of precancers in the inflamed colon, specifically about field cancerisation, the replacement of the colonic epithelium by genetically-altered cancer-predisposed clone(s) without morphological change. I will discuss how detection of the cancerised field strongly predicts future cancer risk and provides a powerful biomarker to enable personalised approaches to cancer prevention.

    *This lecture will be recorded and will be available to view exclusively by Pathological Society members. It will not be available to watch on our YouTube channel.

     

    RCPath CPD Certified Event

    To obtain the CPD Certificate, you must attend the Lecture for at least 55 minutes.
