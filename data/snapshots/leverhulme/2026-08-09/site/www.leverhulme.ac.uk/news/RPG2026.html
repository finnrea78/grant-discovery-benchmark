Important changes to the Research Project Grant scheme in 2026

Pause of Outline Applications and changes to deadlines for Detailed Applications for Research Project Grants in 2026

We want to give applicants and reviewers as much notice as possible of several changes to deadlines for the Research Project Grant scheme for 2026. Applicants should be clear that while dates may change, the Trust maintains its commitment to the scheme, both in terms of level of funding and number of awards.

Background

The Trust was recently advised by its IT supplier (Symplectic) that it will no longer support its existing Grants Management System after December 2025. We will therefore be moving to a new system in 2026 and as part of this need to make changes to our grant deadlines.

We have been advised that it will not be possible to take applications in progress from one system to another, so we will exceptionally need to impose a deadline for receipt of Outline Applications ahead of the go-live of the new system, and delay one of the deadlines for Detailed Applications.

Outline Applications

We will have a deadline for receipt of Outline Applications of 27 February 2026, 4pm. No Outline Applications can be accepted into the current system after this date.

You will be notified of the outcome of your Outline Application by 30 April 2026.


Detailed Applications

If you already have an invitation to submit a Detailed Application, or receive one before 30 November 2025, then the invitation email will tell you the latest date by which you must submit your Detailed Application. Please note, this is not necessarily the same as the closing date for the round, and applicants are welcome to apply earlier.

If you are invited to make a Detailed Application between 1 December 2025 and 30 April 2026, then you must submit this by 1 September 2026. It will not be possible to extend the deadline further, as applications in progress cannot be transferred to the new system.

 

This means that if you are invited to submit your Detailed Application after 1 December 2025  you will have less time than usual to prepare your application. In particular, those submitting their outline application close to the February deadline will have considerably less time to develop and submit their detailed applications. The Trust strongly urges applicants to think carefully about the timelines and workloads involved, especially across holiday periods, and consider delaying their outline application if this may be an issue.

Closing dates for receipt of Detailed Applications

In 2026, the closing dates for detailed applications into the current system will be 4pm on:

  • 1 July 2026, for decision in October 2026, and
  • 1 September 2026, for decision in December 2026.

Further updates will be published here.
