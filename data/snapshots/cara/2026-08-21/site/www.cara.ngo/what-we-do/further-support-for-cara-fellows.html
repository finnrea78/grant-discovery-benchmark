Further Support for Cara Fellows

We focus primarily on helping academics at risk to find temporary refuge, a place where they can share and further develop their skills until they can return home. But we realise that some academics cannot return to their own countries for some time because of the continuing risk, and need support to identify longer-term opportunities in the UK or elsewhere.

For this reason, our teams stay in close contact with Cara Fellows throughout their initial Fellowship period, and have had significant success in helping well-qualified and motivated Fellows to apply for Global Talent visas and to find other ways to take up employment opportunities in universities and elsewhere. We have also been able to help Fellows to identify further opportunities at universities outside the UK.
