Apply Now

Applications for the 2027 Nuffield Farming Scholarships are now open.

Application deadline is at Midday on Thursday 30th July 2026.

Please have a look at the Essential Information page to ensure you meet the eligibility requirements.

To join the informal Potential Scholar Drop-in Sessions, please email Charlotte [email protected]
